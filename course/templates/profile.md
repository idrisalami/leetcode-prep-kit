# Profile

Who you are as a candidate, written during discovery, one answer at a time. Claude reads it at the start of every session. Discovery decides pace, priorities and which blocks get the most time; it doesn't change the diagnostic.

## Target

*The role and level you're applying for, the companies or type of company, the interview format if known, how many coding rounds, and the language the interviews are in (it decides matrix row C9).*

## Timeline

*Interview dates if known, and how many hours per week you have.*

## Goals

*What success looks like (e.g. an offer at `<company>` by `<YYYY-MM-DD>`), and which rounds worry you.*

## Experience

*Years and kind of engineering work, your CS background, and how fluent you are in Python.*

## LeetCode history

*Roughly how many problems you've solved, which topics, the Easy/Medium/Hard mix, and any past technical interviews and how they went.*

## Self-assessment

*The topics you think are strong, and the ones you think are weak.*

## Preferences

*Anything you want changed in the coaching rules in `CLAUDE.md`. A change recorded here overrides the default.*

## Other rounds

*Whether you also face system design or behavioural rounds. Recorded here and flagged as outside this kit.*
